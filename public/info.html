Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Get details on how the CivicIndia portal facilitates grievance redressal using technology.

01 How does the AI image classification model work?

When you upload an image of a civic issue (like garbage piles or pothole spots), our backend passes the image dimensions and textures to a trained Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) neural network model.

The model outputs probability values for 8 standard categories (Garbage, Pothole, Streetlight, Traffic, Water Supply, Vandalism, Drainage, and Other) and automatically suggests the category with the highest confidence level.

02 Can I override the category suggested by the AI?

Yes! If you upload an image and the AI classification result is incorrect, or if you prefer to choose manually, you can simply click "Change Category" and select the appropriate option from the dropdown menu.

03 How does the duplicate prevention check work?

To avoid spamming municipal officers with multiple tickets for the same issue, the application queries other active complaints in the same city. If a ticket with the same category exists, it shows an alert suggesting that you track the existing ticket instead.

04 What are the possible statuses of a complaint?

Every report progresses through a standard workflow visible on the timeline:

  • Pending: Submitted by a citizen and waiting to be assigned to an officer.
  • In Progress: Assigned to a municipal department officer.
  • Resolved: Addressed and resolved by the local municipal authorities.
  • Rejected: Flagged as invalid, spam, or out-of-scope.
