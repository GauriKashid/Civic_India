Key Features

🤖

AI Image Recognition

Upload a picture of the civic issue. Our machine learning classifier instantly identifies the category (e.g. potholes, streetlight, vandalism, garbage) to speed up submission.

📍

GPS Pinpoint Capture

Single-click GPS captures your exact coordinates. Automatic address lookup reverse-geocodes your position using municipal maps for quick dispatch.

🛡️

Duplicate Report Filtering

Prevents spam by checking if another user has reported the same issue in your local neighborhood, letting you watch the existing ticket instead.
